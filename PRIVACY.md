# DocDrop privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

DocDrop is a Chrome extension that opens, edits and presents Word, Excel and PowerPoint files inside your browser. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

DocDrop does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## Your files

- Files are read and displayed entirely on your computer, inside the extension.
- Files are never uploaded anywhere.
- When you save, the file is written to a location on your computer that you choose (or, with your permission, back to the file you opened).

## What DocDrop stores on your computer

- **Settings:** whether automatic opening is on, and your light or dark appearance choice (Chrome extension storage).
- **Recent files:** copies of up to 8 recently opened files (each under 30 MB), so you can reopen them from the recent list. They stay inside the extension on your computer and are never sent anywhere. Use **Clear list** in the DocDrop tab to delete them. Removing the extension deletes them too.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Downloads | To notice when a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file finishes downloading so DocDrop can open it, and to offer a Save As window when saving. |
| Storage | To remember your settings. |
| Active tab | Only when you click "Edit the PDF in this tab": lets DocDrop read the PDF open in that one tab so you can edit it. |
| Access to file URLs (`file:///*`) | To read the file you just downloaded from your computer so it can be shown. Only used for files you download or open. |

## Children

DocDrop does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with a new date.
